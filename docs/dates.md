# Your claim date

GitHub records a date during the automatic submission process, before the
maintainers review your claim. Waiting for review or publishing your work later
does not change that date.

The date relies on GitHub and the registry maintainers. It records your
submission; it does not establish who discovered a result first.

If the work changes, create a new claim. If a submission fails, ask a maintainer
to rerun the original check. Corrections require a new submission.
